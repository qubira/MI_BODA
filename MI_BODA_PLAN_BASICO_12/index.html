Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know

How do we get to the villa?

A private boat will depart from Lenno pier at 2:30 PM. We recommend arriving in Lenno by car or train. Detailed directions will be sent closer to the date.

What should we wear?

Black tie optional. Think elegant summer — long dresses, light suits, and comfortable shoes for garden paths. The ceremony is outdoors, so bring a light wrap for the evening breeze.

What about the weather?

September on Lake Como is typically warm and sunny (22–26°C). We have a stunning indoor backup space in the villa should the weather surprise us.

Can children attend?

We love your little ones! Children are warmly welcome. We'll have a dedicated kids' area with activities and a babysitter available during dinner.

Gifts

Your presence at our celebration on Lake Como is the greatest gift we could ask for. For those who wish to contribute, a gift towards our honeymoon along the Amalfi Coast would bring us immense joy.
